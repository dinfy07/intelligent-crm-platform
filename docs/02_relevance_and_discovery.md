# Relevance evidence and discovery plan

## Evidence status
Confirmed from the team's current statement: there is no real client agreement, no completed user discussion and no supervisor feedback record yet. Published dialogue research and existing CRM documentation establish technical context, but they do not establish local demand for this specific product [L03, L06, L08, S01–S06].

## Hypotheses to test
H1: Representatives repeatedly reconstruct customer context from scattered text records.
H2: They value source-linked summaries and review controls enough to use them in an existing workflow.
H3: Topic labels help locate unresolved issues.
H4: Their organisation can lawfully provide de-identified examples and define who may access them.
H5: English-first text input is sufficient for a demonstrable initial scope. Interview evidence may contradict this.

## Recruitment and sample
Recruit 3–5 representatives from the target workflow, ideally at least two customer-facing employees and one manager. Do not count the three project developers as target users solely because they built the prototype. A single actual client may instead provide substantive agreement on users, scope, data access and acceptance criteria, as permitted by the supplied rubric.

## Discussion guide for 20–25 minutes
1. Describe the last time you reviewed a customer's previous conversation. What triggered the task?
2. Show the steps, tools and handovers without exposing personal or confidential data.
3. What facts do you need before deciding the next action? What happens when one is missing?
4. How often does this task occur? If possible, observe a timed example rather than relying only on estimates.
5. What languages and interaction formats actually occur? Which records are too sensitive to share?
6. Show the prototype. Ask the participant to find an unresolved issue and check a summary against the original.
7. What is incorrect, unnecessary or missing? What would make the proposed workflow unsuitable?
8. Ask what an acceptable result would look like and which mistakes are unacceptable.
9. May the team keep de-identified notes? May an anonymised quote be included? These are separate permissions.

## Record and synthesis
Use participant codes U01–U05. Record date, role, workflow, observed steps, supporting notes, permission scope, contradictory findings and candidate requirement IDs. Separate exact quotations from interpretation. Do not insert invented names, quotes, durations or percentages.

After each discussion, classify a statement as observed, reported, inferred or proposed. Retain conflicting views. Summarise findings by workflow and link a requirement only when the supporting note exists. With 3–5 convenience participants, report qualitative patterns and avoid population prevalence claims.

## Evidence completion gate
Before the checkpoint, attach 3–5 substantive de-identified records or a real client agreement. Update the requirements and prototype in response to findings. Preserve the previous design and a change log. Until this occurs, component 2 remains open.
